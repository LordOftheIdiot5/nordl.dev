Privacy policy

How this independent project portfolio handles visitor information.

Last updated: 5 November 2026. This policy covers the pages served from nordl.dev. Projects hosted on other domains or subdomains may have different processing practices and policies.

Website operator and contact

The website is operated as an independent development portfolio from Norway. For privacy questions, corrections or requests concerning this website, email Loticoins@proton.me.

Hosting and routine technical data

These static pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit, the hosting and network providers may process information such as IP address, browser headers, requested URLs, and security or access logs to provide and protect the website. How long this information is retained depends on the provider's applicable terms and settings. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts and external websites

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your browser's connection details when those resources are requested. External links to GitHub, project demos or third-party tools take you to websites with their own privacy policies. See Google's privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising and cookies

This website currently contains only a Google AdSense publisher-ownership verification meta tag and an ads.txt seller authorization record. Neither one loads advertisements or advertising tracking scripts. We have not enabled AdSense ad serving through these pages. The pages do not currently use a site-operated behavioural analytics service or advertising pixel. Hosting or other embedded services may use essential technical storage under their own terms.

If advertising is enabled later, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant data processing and consent choices. Where required, visitors will receive an appropriate consent management interface before nonessential advertising processing begins.

Contact messages

If you email us, we receive the email address and contents you send. This information is used to answer your message and handle related requests. Please do not include passwords, private keys or other confidential credentials. The email provider processes messages under its own terms. We keep correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry or applicable obligations.

Your rights and updates

Depending on applicable privacy law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, or raise other privacy concerns using the contact address above. You may also contact the relevant data-protection supervisory authority. This page will be reviewed when the website's hosting, tracking, advertising or data collection changes.
